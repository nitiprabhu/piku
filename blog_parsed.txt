Generating an AI video that reflects your imagined concept can be tricky. The narrative doesn’t flow well, the scenes don’t transition correctly, or the entire composition just misses the mark.
The quality of
AI video generation
depends on the quality of prompt. Prompting bridges your imagination and AI video creation. That’s why understanding AI video prompts is important. I have compiled a list of 74 prompts divided into different categories to help you experiment and create production-ready clips.
Essential Prompt Writing Tips
In essence, prompt works as a blueprint for AI video generation. It tells AI video generator what to compose, how to frame, and how to move camera or light the scene. An effective AI video prompt is clear, detailed, and creative. Write an abstract prompt, and AI video tool will struggle to create anything useful. Make your prompt too cluttered, and AI will misinterpret your creative vision.
Here’s a prompt template that you can use for effective AI video prompting:
[Subject/Action] in [Environment/Setting], [Camera angle/movement], [Lighting/Mood], [Style/Genre], [Additional Details/Effects]
However, different AI video models handle prompts in different ways. For example:
Veo 3.1 accepts JSON-style prompts, which lets you structure multiple elements like camera angles, lighting, and effects in a single, organized input.
Seedance 2.0 supports multi-shot prompting, so you can describe several consecutive shots in one prompt for dynamic storytelling.
Kling 3.0 also accepts multi-shot prompts, but it can produce equally great results even with simpler prompts, making it great for beginners or fast iterations.
Here are some best practices for AI video generation prompts:
1. Start With Clarity
Define exactly what’s happening. Have a clear idea about your characters, scenes, and overall the theme and visual style of your AI video.
2. Add Cinematic Language
Include these terms in your AI video prompt:
Slow dolly-in
Tracking shot
Crane shot
Handheld camera
Macro close-up
360° orbit
These camera movements dramatically improve storytelling and add visual depth to your video content.
3. Define Lighting
The realism of your AI video depends heavily on lighting:
Soft diffused lighting
Golden hour glow
Neon cyberpunk lighting
High-contrast studio lighting
4. Specify Visual Style
Mentioning visual style guides AI about the scene composition and desired theme of your AI video. Use words like cinematic, realistic, documentary, editorial, UGC-style, hyper-detailed, etc.

Product Ad Videos
These video prompts help you portray your products with cinematic flair. Experiment with different camera movements and angles to get the best shot of your product. You can use these AI video prompts to as templates or refine them to fit your brand voice.
Create a cinematic luxury skincare advertisement featuring a transparent glass serum bottle placed on polished white marble. Tiny water droplets slide down the bottle surface. Begin with an extreme macro close-up of the brand logo, then perform a slow dolly-out revealing the full product. Use soft diffused studio lighting with subtle golden highlights. Add shallow depth of field, ultra-realistic textures, and elegant minimalist background. Mood: premium, clean, aspirational.
Open on a dark screen with subtle ambient electronic sound. A beam of neon blue light reveals a sleek smartwatch floating in mid-air. Perform a 360° orbit camera movement around the watch while the screen interface animates. Use dramatic rim lighting and cyberpunk color accents (blue and purple). End with a slow zoom-in on the watch face in ultra-detailed 4K.
Show coffee beans falling in slow motion onto a rustic wooden table. Transition into a cinematic close-up of hot coffee being poured into a ceramic mug with steam rising naturally. Use warm golden hour lighting streaming through a window. Include smooth tracking shots and shallow depth of field for realism. Mood: comforting, cozy, morning ritual.
Set on a rooftop at sunset overlooking a city skyline. Start with macro close-ups of sneaker texture and stitching. Cut to a low-angle tracking shot of a model lacing the shoes. Transition into a dynamic running sequence using steady cam motion. Vibrant orange sky lighting, energetic tone.
Black reflective studio floor. Spotlight glides slowly across a crystal perfume bottle. Begin with a crane shot descending from darkness to product level. Use high-contrast lighting and dramatic shadows. Add soft fog in background for depth.
Ultra-modern tech lab environment. Phone rotates slowly in mid-air while holographic UI projections appear around it. Smooth orbit camera movement, cool white lighting, clean aesthetic, hyper-detailed textures.
